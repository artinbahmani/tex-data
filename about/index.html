Why a brokerage publishes its own data

TEX & TEX is a licensed Dubai brokerage. We built this because the honest answer to "what is my property worth" is a set of registered transactions, and because almost nobody in Dubai will show you those without asking for your phone number first.

What this is

Every morning a machine in our office pulls the Dubai Land Department's open records, stores them, and rebuilds this site. 842,621 rows so far. No estimates, no asking prices, no "starting from" numbers. If it is on this site, someone signed for it and the DLD registered it.

How we handle the awkward parts

We say what we cannot seeNo unit numbers, no owner names, no service charges. They are not in open data and we will not estimate them. Every page that hits a limit says so.
We publish our methodMedians not averages. Residential only. Plausibility filters on value and area. Multi-property contracts excluded. It is all written down.
We correct ourselves in publicWe found and fixed four serious data faults before launch, including a duplicate-contract problem in the source feed that inflated rentals by 7%.
No wall, no blurNothing here is gated. The CSVs are free to download and free to republish with credit, because that is what the licence asks and what a reference should do.
