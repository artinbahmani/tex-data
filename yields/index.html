Apartment gross yields, district by district

For every district with enough registered apartment sales and enough registered apartment tenancy contracts, this is the median rent per square foot divided by the median sale price per square foot. Apartments on both sides, so land and whole-building deals cannot distort it. Gross: service charges, agency fees and vacancy are not deducted.
