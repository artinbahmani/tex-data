Dubai's open Ejari register publishes contract-level records with the district, the building or project name, the property type and size, the annual rent and the start and end dates. It does not publish unit numbers, tenant names, landlord names or any contact details, and neither do we. This is a market-timing map at building level, not a contact list. Approaching owners or tenants without a lawful basis is regulated in Dubai and we do not do it.

One more honesty note: the DLD feed repeats a contract once per project name in its district. We collapse those duplicates, and where the real building cannot be determined we count the contract at district level only, so no building number here is inflated.
