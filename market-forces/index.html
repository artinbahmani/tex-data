What we do not publish, and why

Agent and agency counts, and deals per agent. The broker and office registries exist, but attributing a transaction to a named agent sits behind a key-gated DLD endpoint. Other sites publish agent leaderboards; we would be guessing, so we do not.

Construction progress and escrow. These live in DLD's project registry. Our requests to its open endpoint have not been accepted yet. The moment they are, completion percentages appear across the site.

Service charges. Held in Mollak, which is gated to registered management companies. See our note on it.
