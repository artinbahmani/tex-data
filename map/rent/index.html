Where reletting pays, and where it costs

The renewal gap is what a new let is asking today against what the leases now ending were signed at. A positive gap is the headroom a landlord has on renewal. A negative one is the discount the market has already moved to, whatever the sitting rent says.

How to read it

The filter drives the colourPick a window and the map recolours to the leases ending inside it, dimming every district with nothing expiring. The number you see and the number you filtered on are always the same number.
Buildings carry their rentZoom past the district level and each building we track shows its own median rent on a pill. Building attribution is only shown where the DLD feed makes it unambiguous.
Rent per sqft, not just rentA cheap rent in a large unit is not a cheap rent. Switch to Per sqft to compare like with like across unit sizes.
What this cannot tell youThe open feed carries no unit numbers and no names. This is the finest lawful grain there is: the building, the count, and the date the lease ends.
