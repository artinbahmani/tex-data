How to read it

The dotted ring is the comparison setOnly the buildings inside it are in the median you are being shown. Widen or narrow it and the answer changes, which is exactly the point.
Price per sqft firstMedian price compares a studio against a penthouse. Price per sqft is the honest default, and it is what the map opens on.
Short windows moveA 3-month median in a small building can swing on a handful of sales. The card tells you how many sales are behind the number, so you can judge it yourself.
Off-plan supply is the pressureSwitch to Off-plan supply and the pills show how many units each building has sold before completion. A tower with a lot of it will be competing with its own buyers on resale.
