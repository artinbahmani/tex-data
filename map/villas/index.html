Villa yields, where they can be trusted

9 of 140 districts publish a villa yield here. The rest are left blank on purpose: the DLD records villa area as the plot in some districts and the built-up area in others, and dividing one by the other produces a number that looks precise and means nothing. A yield is shown only where both sides have at least 15 records and describe comparably sized homes.

How to read it

Villas only, on both sidesSales are DLD villa transactions and rents are villa tenancies. Nothing here is diluted by apartment activity.
Size is a measure you can mapColour by Size and the map shows where Dubai builds large. It explains most of the price difference between one villa district and the next.
Expiring leases, same filterThe Expiring-in control works exactly as it does on the rental map, cut to villa tenancies.
Blank is honestA district with no villa sales this year is left unpainted. We do not carry a number forward from last year to fill a gap.
