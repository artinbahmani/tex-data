Check construction progress officially

DLD publishes project status, escrow and completion through its own channels. For a specific project, the Dubai REST app and the DLD project enquiry services will tell you the registered status. If you are buying off-plan, verify the escrow account and the project registration before you pay anything, every time, no exceptions.

When the registry endpoint starts answering us, completion percentages and handover quarters appear on this page and on every project page.

Buying into a project under construction?

We will check the escrow, the registration and the developer's delivery record before you sign anything. That check is free and it takes a day.
