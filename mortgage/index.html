How much of Dubai is borrowed

34,684 mortgages worth AED 150,613,858,596 were registered with the Dubai Land Department this year, against 118,883 residential sales. That is roughly one mortgage registration for every 3.4 sales, which tells you how cash-heavy this market still is.

What this does and does not show

These are mortgage registrations on the DLD register, which include new purchase financing, refinancing and equity release, and they are not matched one-to-one against sales. The open feed carries no loan-to-value and no lender name, so we do not publish either. The sales-per-mortgage ratio is a directional signal, not a cash-buyer percentage.
