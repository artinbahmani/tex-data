Why this starts in January 2026

The Dubai Land Department's open feed serves the current calendar year only, and the old Dubai Pulse bulk archive has been retired. We began harvesting every dataset daily onto our own machine, so from here the history is ours and it only gets longer. Anyone showing you a longer free series is using an archive that is no longer published.
